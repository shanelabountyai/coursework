# EVAL — what the TypeScript engines reproduce, as measured

Every number on this page is printed by the command named beside it, on the frozen fixtures (`tools/build_fixtures.py --seed 20261025`). Measured 2026-10-26 on branch `kickoff`.

## What these numbers are, and are not (D-31)

They are **agreement between two implementations of the same stated rules on the generator's own data, by the same author**: `tools/courselib.py` (the oracle, stdlib Python) and `src/graph/` + `src/grades/` (TypeScript), compared row by row over the seven `expected_*.csv` files the oracle wrote. They prove that the TypeScript reproduces rules G1–G9, R1–R3, P1, V1 and S1–S9 as the brief states them. They say nothing about whether 70.00 is the right pass mark, whether redistributing an empty category's weight is fair, whether an OR-branch cycle should be refused, or whether a school's prerequisites should be tightened retroactively; those are O-items in `docs/decisions.md`. No real school, learner, transcript or policy is involved (README banner).

Not generated by the fixtures, so not measured here: incomplete or withdrawn grades, regrades, re-takes with grade replacement, co-requisites, minimum-grade prerequisites, time-limited prerequisites, cohort transfers, concurrent catalog versions for one learner, instructor overrides of a final grade (brief §3).
